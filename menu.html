Allergens & Dietary

Our kitchen handles a variety of ingredients, including common allergens such as wheat, milk, eggs, nuts, soy and shellfish. If you have a food allergy or specific dietary needs, please let your server know and we will do our best to accommodate you. Being Mexican, a lot of our dishes contain coriander.
